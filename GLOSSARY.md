# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers buying courses, getting access, and getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Platform Admin**:
A member of Cadence staff authorised to approve or decline Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), the team, Staff

**Team Admin**:
A learner who buys and manages a Team Licence. This role grants no authority to decide Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own)

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
A single payment for a course. An Individual Purchase gives the buyer course access; a Team Licence provides Seats for others.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase of Seats for other learners, not course access for the buyer.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence, unclaimed until a learner claims it to get course access.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Code, Voucher

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
The buyer's request to get their money back for a Purchase. It is open until a Platform Admin approves or declines it.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
Money returned to the buyer after their Refund Request is approved, not the request or approval itself.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

### Other terms

**Watched**:
The amount of course video a learner has played, not merely lessons they have opened.
_Avoid_: Viewed, Progress
